# Privacy Policy for Kodo

_Last updated: 24 August 2026_

**Kodo does not collect any data.**

There is no account, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no server.
Nothing you scan or create leaves your device.

## What Kodo stores

Every code you scan or create is saved to a history list **on your device only**,
using Apple's SwiftData. It contains the contents of the code, the type of code,
the date, and whether you marked it as a favourite. You can delete any entry by
swiping it, and deleting the app removes all of it.

## Permissions Kodo asks for

| Permission | Why it is used |
| --- | --- |
| **Camera** | To read QR codes and barcodes. Camera frames are processed on your device to find codes and to draw the preview. They are never recorded, stored, or sent anywhere. |
| **Photos (add only)** | Only when you tap Save, so a QR code you created can be written to your photo library. Kodo cannot read your library with this permission. |
| **Photo picker** | When you scan a code from a picture or choose a logo. iOS shows the picker and hands Kodo only the single image you select. |
| **Contacts** | Only when you tap "Add to Contacts" on a scanned contact code, so it can be saved to your address book. Kodo does not read your existing contacts. |
| **Wi-Fi (Hotspot Configuration)** | Only when you tap "Join network" on a scanned Wi-Fi code, so iOS can connect to that network. |

Every one of these is used only at the moment you ask for it.

## Links you open

If you open a link from a scanned code, it opens in your browser and is then
subject to that website's own privacy policy. Kodo checks the link's address
locally first, to warn you about lookalike domains, shortened links, hidden
destinations, and unencrypted connections. This check happens entirely on your
device and involves no network request.

## Children

Kodo collects no data from anyone, including children.
